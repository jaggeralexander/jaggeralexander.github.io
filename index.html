About Me

My name is Jagger Alexander. I work to drive meaningful health interventions by analyzing climate and health data with novel statistical and machine learning techniques. I am currently completing my Ph.D. in Geosciences at the University of Texas at Austin. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from Vanderbilt University in 2020 and my M.S. in Climate and Health from the University of Miami in 2021. I am also proud to be a licensed EMT.
